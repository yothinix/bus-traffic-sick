Depot Dash

Tap a bus whose road is clear to send it to a bay. Riders board buses that match their color; the number on a bus is its seats, and long buses take up two spaces. Crowned VIP riders only ride the gold-trimmed VIP bus of their color. If every bay fills up and the front rider has no matching bus, you're stuck.

Clearing a level earns 10 coins, or 20 if you used no power-ups. Each power-up works once per level. Tow lifts any bus, even a blocked one, into the express lane. Shuffle repaints the buses still in the lot. Rush seats riders from anywhere in line on the buses already in the bays. Extra bay opens one more bay for this level.

Riders who board one after another without a pause make a run. Park buses for riders further back in line first, then send in the bus the front rider needs, and the whole line boards in one go. A run of 10 riders earns 2 coins, 15 earns 6, 20 earns 12, 30 earns 30.
